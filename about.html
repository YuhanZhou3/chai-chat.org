Our language matters. We use “people from the street” more often than “homeless people” because a person’s current housing situation is only one part of who they are. We want our language to leave room for the whole person.
What we do

Walk. Greet. Listen. Check in.

Chai Chat is deliberately simple. The repetition matters: showing up every week lets a one-time encounter become a relationship.

01

Walk together

We meet in Montreal, pair up or stay in small groups, and walk routes where our friends are likely to be.

02

Start a conversation

We say hello without forcing anything. A chat might last two minutes, twenty minutes, or simply be a familiar wave.

03

Keep showing up

We remember names, interests, jokes, worries, and small details when people choose to share them—and check in again later.

The idea

We are not here to “fix” a person.

We are here to make it ordinary to say hello. The aim is not to turn people into projects, but to make human connection less unusual.
